# Generated architecture views

Every file in this folder is produced by `terrane render` from the model in `10_MODEL/`. Editing them is pointless; the next render overwrites them.

Four kinds of thing live here. A **diagram** has a subject and is read closely. A **map** has no subject and is scanned. A **report** answers a question with a set, so it is a table, drawn as one. A **timeline** puts dated things on a calendar of quarters.

A diagram or a map the engine lays out the way the app does is also drawn as the app draws it, as SVG under `svg/`, the provider's marks inside the file and the faces named rather than embedded; the **Drawing** column links it, and each view's page carries it under the Mermaid; a map with no connections is the tiles the app shows. A drawing the app lays out in its browser, by ELK or organically, is drawn the same way here through a browser on the machine; where there was none, the column says which layout the picture needed. A drawing marked † is laid out by dagre where the app would use ELK: the app's own fallback, not its first choice.
